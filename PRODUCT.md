# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Vite + TanStack Start (TS/React) + Tailwind CSS v4

## Users
Patients in Francisco Beltrão (PR) and surrounding region seeking premium, high-quality, and trustworthy dental care (preventive, restorative, implants, and cosmetic), who value a welcoming, human, and modern clinical environment.

## Product Purpose
To serve as a premium, sophisticated digital showcase for Bella Odontologia that educates potential patients and drives high-intent appointment requests/WhatsApp inquiries.

## Positioning
A luxury healthcare experience in Francisco Beltrão that combines rigorous clinical excellence (led by Dra. Danielle C. Lourenço, CRO-PR 22533) with a deeply personalized, humanized, and comfortable treatment journey.

## Operating Context
- Primarily accessed by mobile users on-the-go or looking for contact info.
- Focuses on conversion via WhatsApp and appointment request forms.
- Requires instant loading, high trust signals, and elegant readability.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Booking/scheduling CTA actions routing to WhatsApp and a dedicated appointment section.
- Clear breakdown of clinic specialties, treatments, and clinical structure.
- Must preserve existing, verified functional features (appointment forms, contact info, and WhatsApp deep links).
- Hard constraint: Do not invent stats, patient reviews, awards, credentials, or additional dentist info.

## Brand Commitments
- **Name**: Bella Odontologia
- **Colors**: Deep burgundy/wine, warm ivory, champagne/gold accents.
- **Typography**: Elegant editorial serif typography paired with a clean, highly readable sans-serif.
- **Tone & Voice**: Human, trustworthy, refined, authoritative yet welcoming, modern and sophisticated.
- **Tone Boundary**: Avoid clinical sterility, generic templates, excessive card layouts, futuristic dental aesthetics, and plastic-looking AI imagery.

## Evidence on Hand
- **Registered Professional**: Dra. Danielle C. Lourenço (CRO-PR 22533)
- **Verified Location**: R. Ver. Romeu Lauro Werlang, 822 - Centro, Francisco Beltrão - PR, 85601-020
- **Google Reviews**: 5.0 average based on 12 verified reviews.
- **Existing Assets**: Local clinical structure photos (`estrutura-clinica.jpg`, etc.) and real photos of the leading dentist (`dra-danielle.jpeg`).

## Product Principles
1. **Human & Refined Elegance**: The visual presentation must feel like an inviting, high-end healthcare experience rather than a sterile clinic or a generic web template.
2. **Absolute Transparency & Trust**: Every claim, credential, review, and treatment detail must be factually accurate and trace directly to verified clinic operations.
3. **Slick & Spacious Conversion**: Every interaction—especially on mobile—must lead effortlessly toward a WhatsApp discussion or a direct appointment request, utilizing clear visual hierarchy and generous spacing.
